Voice Presence for Discord puts your voice channel on the Stream Deck Neo
Infobar. See up to four participant avatars at a glance, with a green ring
around the person speaking and red badges for muted or deafened members.

The plugin also announces arrivals, departures, and channel changes, and
reminds you while your own microphone or headset is muted. It works alongside
the Discord plugin from Elgato: Discord keeps its mute, deafen, and join keys,
while Voice Presence fills the Infobar.

## Features

- Live participant avatars on the Stream Deck Neo Infobar.
- Green speaking ring around the active speaker.
- Red mute and deafen badges.
- Arrival, departure, and channel-change announcements.
- Automatic scrolling when more than four people are in the channel.
- Configurable background color from the Property Inspector.
- Reconnect action for renewing the Discord authorization.

## Requirements

- Stream Deck 7.6 or later, with a Stream Deck Neo.
- Discord for Windows, running and signed in.
- A one-time Discord authorization (the plugin opens the consent window).

## Notes

- Only the voice channel you are currently connected to is visible.
- The Infobar is display-only; the plugin does not add touch controls.
- Voice Presence uses Discord's local RPC. It does not use a bot, an account
  token, or web scraping.
